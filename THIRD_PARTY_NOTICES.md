# GeoNames cities data

AutoDark Redux includes a reduced offline city index derived from the GeoNames geographical database.

- Source: https://www.geonames.org/
- Download: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip
- Snapshot: 2026-09-02
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Changes: entries were filtered to populated places with population at least 50,000 or administrative-capital feature codes; fields and alternate names were reduced; ordering was changed.
