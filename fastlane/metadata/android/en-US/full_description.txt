AutoDark Redux - Schedule dark mode with Material You.

A modernized fork of 0ranko0P/AutoDark, now for Android 12-16.

<b>What's new in Redux:</b>
* Material 3 + Dynamic Color (Monet) - App follows wallpaper colors
* Themed Icons - launcher icon tints with wallpaper (Android 13+, enforced on 16)
* Dual wallpaper - like iOS, separate light/dark wallpapers auto-switch
* Edge-to-Edge + Compose - rebuilt main screen with Material 3 Expressive
* SDK 36 (Android 16) - AGP 8.7.3 / Kotlin 2.0.20 / Gradle 8.11

<b>Requirements:</b>
* AOSP Android 12-16 (API 29-36)
* WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS (adb grant)
* Shizuku optional, only for Live Wallpapers
